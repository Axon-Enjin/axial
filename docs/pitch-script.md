[SLIDE 2 — The Problem] (~25s)

Philippine MSMEs aren't failing because they're bad at business.

They're cash-trapped.

Buyers pay Net 60, Net 90. The law says pay your people every two weeks.

A growing company can look healthy and still run out of cash.

Then there's compliance.

By December 2026, BIR Electronic Invoicing goes live — file within three days or face penalties.

More than half of MSMEs still run statutory payroll on spreadsheets.

Two crises. No tool fixes both.

[advance]
